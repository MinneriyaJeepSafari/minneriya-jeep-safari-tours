# 🐘 Kaudulla National Park Safari

## Overview

Discover the incredible wildlife of Kaudulla National Park, known for its large elephant populations, diverse birdlife, and beautiful natural surroundings. Kaudulla is often the best alternative when elephants migrate between parks.

## Highlights

* Large herds of wild elephants
* Bird watching paradise
* Peaceful natural environment
* Expert local safari guide
* Great photography opportunities

## Tour Includes

* Private Safari Jeep
* Professional Driver Guide
* Hotel Pickup & Drop
* Wildlife Tracking Assistance
* Comfortable Safari Experience

## Best Time to Visit

August to December

## Duration

3–4 Hours

## Location

Kaudulla National Park, Sri Lanka
