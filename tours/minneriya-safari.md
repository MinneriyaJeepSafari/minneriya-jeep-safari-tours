# 🐘 Minneriya National Park Safari

## Overview

Experience the world-famous Elephant Gathering at Minneriya National Park, one of Sri Lanka's most spectacular wildlife events. Explore beautiful landscapes and observe large herds of Asian elephants in their natural habitat.

## Highlights

* Witness wild Asian elephants
* Comfortable 4x4 safari jeep
* Professional local guide
* Bird watching opportunities
* Scenic reservoir views
* Family-friendly wildlife experience

## Tour Includes

* Private Jeep Safari
* Experienced Driver Guide
* Hotel Pickup & Drop
* Park Entry Assistance
* Wildlife Spotting

## Best Time to Visit

July to October is the peak season for the famous Elephant Gathering.

## Duration

3–4 Hours

## Location

Minneriya National Park, Sri Lanka
