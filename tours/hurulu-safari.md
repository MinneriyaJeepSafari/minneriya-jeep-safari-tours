# 🌿 Hurulu Eco Park Safari

## Overview

Explore the natural beauty of Hurulu Eco Park, a protected biosphere reserve offering unforgettable wildlife experiences. This eco-friendly safari destination is perfect for elephant sightings and nature lovers seeking a peaceful adventure.

## Highlights

* Wild elephant encounters
* Eco-tourism experience
* Beautiful forest landscapes
* Rich biodiversity
* Excellent wildlife photography

## Tour Includes

* Private Safari Jeep
* Local Wildlife Guide
* Hotel Pickup & Drop
* Nature Interpretation
* Personalized Safari Experience

## Best Time to Visit

Year-round depending on elephant movement.

## Duration

3–4 Hours

## Location

Hurulu Eco Park, Sri Lanka
